Core Objectives
 Build a robust data-driven demand planning framework to:
. Support S&OP decision-making
. Understand market dynamics by SKU, country, and channel
. Build executive dashboards for supply chain leaders

Tools being used:
Excel, Power BI, SQL Server

Analysis Process
* Cleaned and prepared the data using Excel, XLOOKUP/VLOOKUP and Power Query.
* Performed EDA on sales trends, top SKUs and country performance.
* Conducted ABC and XYZ analysis to assess product contribution and demand variability.
* Used correlation analysis to examine relationships between price, promotions and sales volume.
* Built a Power BI S&OP dashboard to visualize key business metrics.

Key Findings
* Seasonality: July recorded the highest monthly sales, while February was the lowest. Week 1 had the highest weekly sales.
* Top Product: SKU 0018 recorded the highest sales/revenue.
* ABC Analysis: A-class SKUs were concentrated in Beverages, Dairy, Home Care, Personal Care and Snacks.
* Demand Variability: Demand showed substantial variation across the periods analyzed.
* Correlation: Price had a very weak negative relationship with units sold (r = -0.0785), while promotions had a weak positive relationship (r = 0.2985). 

Insights & Recommendations
 * Prepare inventory and supply plans ahead of peak periods such as July.
*  Closely monitor high-contribution A-class SKUs and top performers such as SKU 0018.
*  Use demand variability insights to support inventory planning and SKU prioritization. 
*  Monitor promotional periods and compare sales performance to better understand their impact on demand.
